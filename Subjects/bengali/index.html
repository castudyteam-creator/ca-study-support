Bengali

Structured Bengali language tutoring for clear communication

About Bengali Tutoring

Our Bengali tutoring focuses on developing confident communication skills through a structured approach to grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and cultural context.

Lesson Structure

  • Script (Bangla) reading and writing
  • Pronunciation and phonetics
  • Grammar fundamentals and sentence structure
  • Vocabulary building for everyday use
  • Listening comprehension and speaking practice

Who This Is For

  • Beginners with no prior knowledge
  • Heritage speakers seeking literacy support
  • Students learning Bengali for academic or personal reasons

Resources

Students receive customised worksheets, reading passages, vocabulary lists, and guided exercises tailored to their level and learning goals.
