About Biology Tutoring

Our Biology tutoring focuses on developing strong conceptual understanding, accurate scientific language, and exam-ready application skills across key biological systems and processes.

Lesson Structure

  • Core concept explanation using diagrams and models
  • Application to exam-style and data-based questions
  • Scientific terminology and definition precision
  • Revision strategies and active recall techniques
  • Practice questions with targeted feedback

Key Topics Covered

  • Cell structure and function
  • Biomolecules and enzymes
  • Photosynthesis and cellular respiration
  • Genetics, DNA, and inheritance
  • Homeostasis and body systems
  • Evolution and natural selection
  • Ecology and ecosystems

Who This Is For

  • Secondary school biology students
  • Students preparing for exams and assessments
  • Students needing support with scientific writing and data analysis
