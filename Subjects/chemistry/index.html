About Chemistry Tutoring

Our Chemistry tutoring focuses on building conceptual understanding, problem-solving skills, and practical application through structured lessons and targeted exercises.

  • Practical problem-solving and worked examples

  • Exam preparation and past paper practice
  • Concept reinforcement through quizzes and exercises

Key Topics Covered

  • Atoms, molecules, and chemical bonding
  • Stoichiometry and chemical calculations
  • Acids, bases, and pH
  • Chemical kinetics and equilibrium
  • Organic chemistry basics
  • Thermochemistry and energy changes
  • Electrochemistry and redox reactions

Who This Is For

  • Secondary school chemistry students
  • Students preparing for exams or assessments
  • Students needing help with chemical reasoning and calculations
