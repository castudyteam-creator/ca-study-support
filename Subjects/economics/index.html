Economics

Structured tutoring to master microeconomics, macroeconomics, and data analysis

About Economics Tutoring

Our Economics tutoring program helps students understand core economic principles, develop analytical skills, and apply theory to real-world scenarios.

Lesson Structure

  • Core concept explanation and discussion
  • Graphical analysis and problem-solving
  • Application to case studies and current events
  • Exam preparation and past paper practice
  • Data interpretation and quantitative analysis

Key Topics Covered

  • Microeconomics: supply, demand, and market equilibrium
  • Macroeconomics: GDP, inflation, and unemployment
  • Fiscal and monetary policy
  • International trade and finance
  • Economic growth and development
  • Market failures and government intervention

Who This Is For

  • High school and pre-university students
  • Students preparing for exams or assessments
  • Students needing help with economic reasoning and data analysis

Resources

Students receive worksheets, practice questions, case studies, and structured summaries tailored to their curriculum and learning level.
