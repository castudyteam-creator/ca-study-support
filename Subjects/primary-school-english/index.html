Primary School English

Build foundational reading, writing, and comprehension skills

About Primary English Tutoring

Our Primary English tutoring helps young learners develop essential skills in reading, writing, spelling, and comprehension, providing a strong foundation for future learning.

Lesson Structure

  • Phonics and spelling practice
  • Reading fluency and comprehension exercises
  • Writing: sentence structure, paragraphs, and creative writing
  • Grammar and punctuation fundamentals
  • Storytelling, discussion, and vocabulary development

Who This Is For

  • Primary school students needing support in reading and writing
  • Students aiming to improve literacy skills for school assessments
  • Young learners who benefit from structured, one-on-one guidance

Resources

Students receive worksheets, reading passages, writing prompts, and interactive exercises tailored to their skill level and learning goals.
