Primary School Maths

Develop foundational numeracy skills and problem-solving confidence

About Primary Maths Tutoring

Our Primary Maths tutoring helps young learners build a strong foundation in numeracy, problem-solving, and mathematical reasoning, preparing them for success in school and beyond.

Lesson Structure

  • Number sense, counting, and basic operations
  • Fractions, decimals, and percentages
  • Introduction to geometry and measurement
  • Word problems and applied problem-solving
  • Games and activities to reinforce mathematical concepts

Who This Is For

  • Primary school students seeking to improve confidence in maths
  • Students needing support with school assignments or tests
  • Young learners who benefit from structured, one-on-one guidance

Resources

Students receive worksheets, practice exercises, interactive games, and step-by-step explanations tailored to their level and learning goals.
