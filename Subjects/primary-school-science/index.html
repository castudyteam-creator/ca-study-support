Primary School Science

Explore the wonders of science through hands-on learning and discovery

About Primary Science Tutoring

Our Primary Science tutoring introduces young learners to foundational scientific concepts and inquiry skills, encouraging curiosity, observation, and experimentation.

Lesson Structure

  • Introduction to basic scientific concepts (physics, chemistry, biology)
  • Hands-on experiments and observation exercises
  • Developing scientific vocabulary and reasoning
  • Problem-solving and applying the scientific method
  • Interactive activities to reinforce learning and engagement

Who This Is For

  • Primary school students interested in science exploration
  • Students needing support with school science topics
  • Young learners who benefit from structured guidance and experiments

Resources

Students receive worksheets, experiment instructions, observation journals, and guided exercises tailored to their level and learning goals.
