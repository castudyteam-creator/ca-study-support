Psychology

Develop understanding of human behaviour, cognition, and research methods

About Psychology Tutoring

Our Psychology tutoring focuses on understanding key psychological concepts, exploring research methods, and applying theory to human behaviour in various contexts.

Lesson Structure

  • Introduction to core psychological concepts and theories
  • Study of cognitive, biological, and social processes
  • Application through case studies and practical examples
  • Research methods, experiments, and data interpretation
  • Exam and assessment preparation with guided practice

Key Topics Covered

  • Biological psychology and neuroscience
  • Cognition: memory, learning, and perception
  • Developmental and social psychology
  • Research methods and statistics in psychology
  • Abnormal psychology and mental health
  • Application of psychology in real-world contexts

Who This Is For

  • High school or pre-university students studying psychology
  • Students preparing for exams, assessments, or extended responses
  • Students seeking support in understanding theory, research, and applications

Resources

Students receive worksheets, case studies, research summaries, and guided exercises aligned to their curriculum and learning objectives.
